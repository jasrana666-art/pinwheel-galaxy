FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Does it need WebGL or a 3D library?▾

No. The galaxy is pure Canvas 2D math — polar coordinates, additive blending, requestAnimationFrame. It runs in any browser made this decade, including low-power phones, and it never asks for a GPU context.

How heavy is it?▾

One HTML file, roughly 14 KB, zero network requests after fonts. Compare that to a 4K background video loop — this is ~300× lighter and it never bands or compresses.

Will my AI really rebuild it from a prompt?▾

Yes — the prompt is structured, unambiguous language, not vibes. It specifies particle count, rotation model, palette stops, motion behaviour and container format. Paste it into Claude, Cursor, v0 or Copilot and you get a working scene back.

Can I put content over it?▾

That's the point. The galaxy sits behind your hero at subtle luminance — text stays readable because the core glow is composed around a dark centre, not through it. Drop opacity if you want it quieter.

Is it accessible?▾

Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion — animations collapse to near-zero for users who ask for calm. The canvas is decorative and aria-hidden; nothing structural depends on it.
